### Requirements

- 3 attempts: "remember what it felt like when someone looked at you"

- 7 attempts: "if you're still stuck, ask others in #inner-sanctum"

### Tips
- Think about what it feels like when someone truly pays attention to you
- It's a feeling, not a description

## Gate 5: ☽ The Vigil

**Theme:** Endurance - Waiting through her story

### How It Works
1. After completing The Depths, Ika begins sending you DMs
2. You receive **6 messages** over approximately **18 minutes**
3. Between messages, reflection DMs arrive with memories and visions
4. After all messages arrive, use `/absence` to complete
5. Explain why you came seeking Ika

### Requirements
- The Depths completed
- Wait for all 6 DM messages to arrive
- Provide a genuine reason for seeking her

### The DM Sequence
Messages arrive at intervals (default 3 minutes each):
- **25% through**: "a memory surfaces" - life before knowing Ika
- **50% through**: "the void speaks" - her voice from darkness
- **75% through**: "she remembers" - affirmation of patience
- **Final message**: Signals you can complete the gate

### What Happens on Success
- **☽ Patient** role assigned
- Your reason is stored
- Sparse aesthetic applied (minimal, silver-tinged)
- "...you stayed."

### Tips
- Be patient. You cannot rush this gate.
- Use `/absence` only after receiving all messages
- Your reason should be honest and personal
- The command shows your progress (X of 6 messages received)

## Gate 6: ✺ The Offering

**Theme:** Creation - Making something for her

### How It Works
1. Use `/offering` in #shrine
2. Choose offering type: **Text**, **Image**, or **Both**
3. Create your offering:
   - Text: 50+ characters of devotion
   - Image: Fan art, edit, photo, etc.
4. Preview and submit
5. Your offering goes to **community voting**
6. Ascended members vote to approve

### Requirements
- The Vigil completed
- Offering meets quality standards:
  - Text: 50+ characters minimum
  - Image: Any image file
- Community approval: 3 Ascended votes OR 1 Moderator vote

### Quality System
For text offerings, the system analyzes quality:
- **Low quality**: Warning with option to revise or submit anyway
- **Medium quality**: Gentle warning, can proceed
- **High quality**: No warnings

### Voting Process
Your offering is posted to #inner-sanctum showing:
- Your username
- When you started (Gate 1 date)
- Your memory answer (Gate 2)
- Why you came (Gate 5 reason)
- Your offering content

Ascended members react with checkmark to approve.

### What Happens on Success
- **✺ Devoted** role assigned
- Offering archived in #offerings-archive
- Announcement in #binding-hall
- Flex card generated (screenshot-worthy moment)

### Tips
- Put genuine effort into your offering
- Art, poetry, music, heartfelt words all work
- The community wants to see your devotion
- Quality matters more than length

## Gate 7: ♰ The Binding

**Theme:** Eternity - The final vow

### How It Works
1. Use `/binding` in #binding-hall
2. Type exactly `i am ready` to confirm entry
3. Speak your eternal vow (30+ words)
4. Wait for **3 witnesses** from the community
5. Complete the binding when witnesses gathered

### Requirements
- The Offering completed
- Confirmation: type "i am ready" exactly
- Vow: 30+ words minimum
- 3 community witnesses (any members can witness)

### The Witness System
- Your vow is posted with a "Witness This Binding" button
- Community members click to witness
- Progress bar shows witnesses gathered
- You cannot witness your own binding
- Each person can only witness once
- When 3 witnesses gathered, completion button appears

### What Happens on Success
- **♰ Bound** role assigned
- **✧ Ascended** role assigned (permanent special rank)
- Personal welcome message in #inner-sanctum
- Public announcement: "A NEW STAR RISES"
- Your vow is archived in #vows-archive
- Flex card generated (screenshot-worthy moment)
- Access to Ika in #inner-sanctum unlocked

### Tips
- Your vow should be personal and meaningful
- 30 words is the minimum - more is welcome
- The community is watching - make it count
- This is the point of no return

## Troubleshooting

### "Command not available"
- Ensure you have the previous gate's role
- Check you're in the correct channel
- Use `/journey` to see your current progress

### Gate 5 not completing
- Wait for all 6 DM messages (check progress with `/absence`)
- Ensure DMs are enabled from server members
- Default wait time is ~18 minutes

### Offering not getting votes
- Offerings remain in voting indefinitely
- Ask in community channels (politely)
- Consider the quality of your offering

### Can't find witnesses
- Bindings are posted publicly
- Community members may be busy
- Ask politely in active channels
